# Garagely Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 October 2026

Garagely ("Garagely", "we", "us") is a mobile app that helps vehicle owners in Kenya find trusted garages, book services, and track jobs. This policy explains what personal data the Garagely app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have.

It applies to the Garagely app for Android (package `com.garagely.app`) and the services behind it. We process personal data in line with the Kenya **Data Protection Act, 2019**.

If you have a question about this policy or your data, email **garagelyapp@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Data we collect

### Data you give us

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | When you create your account | Shown to garages on your bookings |
| **Email address** | When you sign up with email, or taken from your Google account | Signing in, account verification codes, contacting you about your account |
| **Password** | Email sign-up only | Signing in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see or store them |
| **Phone number** | When you create your account | So a garage you book with can call you about the job |
| **Vehicle details** — make, model, year, engine, number plate, mileage, service dates | When you add a car | Matching services and estimates to your car, and telling the garage which car is coming in |
| **Vehicle photos** | Only if you choose to add them | Showing your car's condition on your profile and bookings |
| **Bookings** — services chosen, date and time, pickup choice, notes, job status and final amount | When you book | Running the booking with the garage and keeping your service history |
| **Reviews and ratings** | Only if you leave one | Shown publicly on the garage's profile, with your name |
| **Preferred payment method** (e.g. cash or M-Pesa) | If you set one | Showing the garage how you plan to pay |

### Data collected automatically

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| **Precise location** (GPS), only while you use the app and only if you allow it | Showing garages near you, sorting them by distance, and the map and directions on the job tracker. Your location is used on your device and sent to Google Maps to draw the map and directions — **we do not store your location on our servers**. If you refuse location access, the app uses a default location in Nairobi instead. |
| **Device push token** (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Sending you notifications about your bookings and account |
| **Account identifiers** (your Firebase user ID) | Linking your data to your account |

We do **not** collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, files, microphone, or advertising ID. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

### Camera and photos
If you add vehicle photos, the app asks to use your camera or photo library. Only the photos you pick are uploaded. You can remove them at any time.

---

## 2. How we use your data

- Creating and securing your account, including verifying your email address with a one-time code
- Finding and showing garages near you
- Sending your booking to the garage you choose, and tracking the job through to completion
- Sending notifications about your bookings and account
- Keeping your service history and receipts
- Showing your reviews on garage profiles
- Preventing fraud and abuse, and keeping the service secure
- Fixing problems and improving the app
- Meeting legal obligations

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

---

## 3. Who we share it with

**Garages you book with.** A garage receives your name, phone number, the details of the vehicle for that booking, and anything you write in the booking. It does not see your other vehicles, your other bookings, or your email address.

**Other users.** Your name and the text of any review you post are visible to other Garagely users on that garage's profile.

**Service providers** that run Garagely for us, and only to provide their service:

| Provider | What it does for us |
|---|---|
| **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) | Accounts and sign-in, storing app data and photos, server functions, push notifications |
| **Google Sign-In** | Signing in with your Google account, if you choose to |
| **Google Maps Platform** | Maps, garage locations, and directions |
| **Google Gmail** | Delivering account verification emails |

These services are run by Google LLC. Data may be processed on servers outside Kenya, including in the United States. Google applies its own safeguards, described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

**Payments.** Where in-app payment is offered, it is processed by the payment provider (such as Safaricom M-Pesa). We receive a payment reference and status — never your M-Pesa PIN or full card details.

**Legal reasons.** We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, garages, or Garagely.

**Business transfers.** If Garagely is merged or sold, your data may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

---

## 4. How long we keep it

- **Account data** (name, email, phone, vehicles, photos) is kept while your account is open.
- **When you delete your account**, we delete your profile, vehicles and photos within **30 days**.
- **Booking and payment records** may be kept for up to **7 years** after the job where Kenyan tax or consumer law requires it. These are kept only for that purpose and are not used for anything else.
- **Verification codes** expire after 10 minutes and are deleted once used. Only a hash of each code is stored.

---

## 5. Your rights and choices

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

- be told how your data is used (this policy);
- get a copy of your personal data;
- correct data that is wrong — you can edit your name, phone number and vehicles in the app at any time;
- have your data deleted;
- object to, or restrict, a particular use of your data;
- withdraw consent, for example to location or notifications, at any time in your device settings.

To use any of these rights, email **garagelyapp@gmail.com** from the email address on your account. We reply within **30 days**. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)** — [www.odpc.go.ke](https://www.odpc.go.ke).

### Deleting your account
To delete your Garagely account and its data, email **garagelyapp@gmail.com** from your account's email address with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law (see section 4).

---

## 6. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and is stored on Google Cloud, which encrypts it at rest. Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us. Server credentials are kept on our servers and never shipped inside the app. Access to user data is limited to what is needed to run and support the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

---

## 7. Children

Garagely is intended for adults aged **18 and over**. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the effective date above, and if a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
